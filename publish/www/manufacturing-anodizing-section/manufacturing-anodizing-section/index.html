Anodizing

A fully automated anodizing plant with Pulse rectifiers — delivering smooth, durable, colour-stable finishes that stand up to exterior use.

Anodizing Section

Anodizing is a finishing process on aluminium in which the visual appearance of the metal is changed to be aesthetically pleasing. This finish must have colour stability and durability — particularly for exterior use.

DHAKATHAI ALCOMAXX PLC's new fully automated anodizing plant uses the latest technology with Pulse rectifiers to give a smooth surface finish with longevity, and anodizes the aluminium sections according to the American Architectural Manufacturing Association (AAMA).

The result is a hard, corrosion-resistant oxide layer with a film thickness of 10–15 microns — providing both the protective performance and the refined look that architectural and industrial profiles demand.
