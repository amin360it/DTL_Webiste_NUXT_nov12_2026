Managing Director or Chief Executive Officer

Application Deadline: April 10, 2025

Requirements

Education: Master of Business Administration (MBA) from any renowned university.

Experience: The applicants should have minimum 10 years practical experience in the Aluminium Profile Manufacturing Company in the senior position.

Additional Requirements

  • Age 45 to 55 years
  • A proven track record, in successfully managing revenue operations team.
  • A high level of business awareness and acumen.
  • Excellent leadership, communication & organizational skills.

Responsibility

  • Give strategic advice and report results and findings to chairman and board members.
  • Manage resources, including the attracting, hiring, firing and retention of personnel.
  • Ensure employees move in the same strategic direction to achieve its mission.
  • Create and report on business plans, monitoring its efficiency and progress.
  • Remain close to all stakeholders including the chairman and board members, staff, clients, key-decision makers and external service suppliers.
  • Research and implement new initiatives to drive revenue, lower operating costs while maintain quality products that are competitive, all while delivery excellent customer support.
  • Continuous monitoring of the annual budget and ensure that revenue/sales targets are met.
  • Manage and report on the effective implementation of a marketing strategy to maintain market relevance and promote products and services to increase sales.
  • Manage key personnel, clients and service providers.
  • Maintain the quality of products, services, customer support and level of service in line with service level agreements and other retention strategies.
  • Promote the wellness of staff and implementing policies in line with labor legislation and health and safety guidelines to create a diverse and positive working environment.
  • Preparation of annual reports and attending/presenting at board meetings.

MIS Manager (Management Information Systems Manager)

Application Deadline: 23 Jan 2025

Requirements - Education

Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science

Experience

At least 8 years - the applicants should have experience in the following business area(s): Manufacturing (Light Engineering and Heavy Industry)

Additional Requirements

  • Age 30 to 40 years
  • Minimum of 8 years of experience in an MIS or IT-related role. Especially manager role at least 4 years.
  • Proven experience in designing, implementing, and maintaining MIS systems in medium to large manufacturing.
  • Experience in data management, reporting tools, and analytics platforms.
  • Strong experience handling hardware, network, and software-related tasks independently.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to delivering high-quality work.
  • Ability to stay up to date with emerging technologies and industry best practice

Responsibilities & Context

The MIS Manager is responsible for managing and optimizing the organization’s information systems, ensuring data accuracy, creating reports, and leading system development. This role involves overseeing ERP system implementation across the organization and management reporting.

  • System Design, Development, and Implementation: Lead the design, development, and implementation of scalable MIS systems; collaborate with business units to translate requirements into technical solutions; oversee the management of databases, reporting tools, and dashboards.
  • Data Management and Reporting: Collect, organize, and manage data from various business units for reporting and analytics; ensure data accuracy, integrity, and security; create and maintain custom reports, dashboards, and KPIs; generate weekly, monthly, and ad-hoc reports.
  • Project Management and Delivery: Manage MIS-related projects from initiation to completion; develop project plans, allocate resources, and set milestones; coordinate with cross-functional teams; manage vendor relationships.
  • MIS System Maintenance and Optimization: Assess performance and reliability of existing systems; troubleshoot, debug, and resolve issues; work with the IT team to optimize systems.
  • Data Security and Compliance: Ensure all MIS systems comply with legal, regulatory, and internal policy standards; implement and maintain data security protocols; perform regular audits.
  • Team Leadership and Development: Train and guide staff on IT skills; organize workshops and training sessions.
  • Collaboration and Stakeholder Management: Maintain strong communication with department heads; act as the primary liaison between IT and business units.
  • Resource Management: Assist in preparing and managing resources; manage procurement of software, tools, and technologies within the allocated budget.
  • Continuous Improvement: Drive ongoing process improvements and automation; keep up to date with industry trends.
  • Hardware, Network, and Software Management: Oversee all hardware, network, and software-related activities; independently manage installation, configuration, and maintenance of network and software systems.

Skills & Expertise

Advanced use of Microsoft Word and Excel, Cloud Technologies, Database management, Hardware & Networking, Leadership and Good Communication skill, programming language, Project Management, Strong Business Acumen, Time Management skills, Training & Development

Workplace: Work at officeEmployment Status: Full TimeGender: Only MaleJob Location: Dhaka

Mechanical In-charge

Date: 09/12/24Job Location: Savar / AshuliaAge Limit: At least 35

Responsibilities

  • Maintenance Strategy: Develop and implement maintenance strategies to maximize machinery uptime and productivity. Design preventive and maintenance schedules for mechanical equipment.
  • Team Leadership: Lead and manage the maintenance team, including training, mentoring, and performance evaluations. Coordinate with other departments to ensure smooth operations.
  • Equipment Maintenance: Oversee the maintenance and repair of mechanical systems and equipment, such as Hydraulic cylinders, Extrusion Machines, conveyors, compressors, pumps, and HVAC systems. Ensure compliance with safety standards and operational guidelines.
  • Budget Management: Prepare and manage the maintenance department budget. Optimize costs through effective inventory and procurement strategies.
  • Technical Troubleshooting: Analyze and resolve mechanical failures and malfunctions in a timely manner. Develop solutions for recurring technical issues to improve system reliability.
  • Documentation and Reporting: Maintain detailed records of maintenance schedules, repairs, and inspections. Generate regular reports on equipment performance and maintenance activities.
  • Safety and Compliance: Enforce adherence to health, safety, and environmental regulations. Conduct risk assessments and implement safety measures.
  • Innovation and Upgrades: Evaluate new technologies and equipment to enhance production efficiency. Recommend and oversee equipment upgrades or replacements as needed.
  • Vendor and Contractor Coordination: Collaborate with external contractors and suppliers for specialized maintenance tasks. Ensure timely procurement of spare parts and tools.
  • Emergency Response: Develop contingency plans for mechanical failures to minimize downtime. Lead the team in responding to emergencies and critical equipment breakdowns.

Desired Skills

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • Proficiency in using maintenance management software (e.g., CMMS).
  • Excellent leadership and communication skills.
  • In-depth knowledge of mechanical systems and safety regulations.

Required Educational Qualification & Experience

  • Preferred: B.Sc Engineering in mechanical minimum 10 years / Diploma in Mechanical minimum 15 years
  • Thai Aluminum Industry (Exp. Extrusion, Anodizing, etc.) - Tall & Good Health
  • Age Limit: At least 35
  • Job Location: Savar / Ashulia
