About DHAKATHAI ALCOMAXX PLC

We are a dynamic, passionate, and industry-leading team committed to excellence in aluminium extrusion. Since our inception in 1997, we have grown into Bangladesh’s largest single manufacturer of aluminium profiles, operating with eight advanced extrusion presses. Our journey has been defined by innovation, quality, and an unwavering dedication to serving diverse industries, including architecture, transportation, hardware, and industrial applications.

What We Produce — Our cutting-edge extrusion lines manufacture a comprehensive range of aluminium profiles tailored for windows, doors, LED lighting, ladders, pipes, curtain walls, and much more. We offer premium finishes, including anodized (silver, champagne, bronze, black, and various customized colors), powder coating, and wood grain textures in various shades — ensuring durability, aesthetics, and superior quality in every product.

Who We Serve — With an extensive dealer network across Bangladesh, we have established ourselves as the preferred choice for high-quality aluminium solutions. We are proudly enlisted with esteemed organizations such as the Military Engineering Services (MES), Public Works Department (PWD), The Aluminum Association, Aluminium Extruders Council, BUET, and numerous developers and REHAB member companies.

Our Strengths

State-of-the-Art Facilities

Our facilities position us at the forefront of the aluminum industry with advanced technologies and ISO 9001:2015 certification.

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Upholding the highest standards of quality and efficiency in every aspect of production.

Hot-Top Casting

Continuous casting billet furnace with hot top technology for prime quality billets using alloy 6063 imported homogenized billet.

8 Computerized Presses

Fully computerized automatic extrusion plants with Japanese PLC controls and dies up to 10” cross section.

In-House Die Facility

High-precision CNC wire-cut, CNC milling & EDM machines to make custom-designed quality dies.

Robotic Powder Coating

First conveyor robotic coating plant in Bangladesh, equipped for a wide range of colors and textures.

Italian Anodizing Plant

Automatic anodizing plant with PULSE technology for ultra-smooth satin, matt and glossy finishes up to 10–15 microns.

Production Facility Features

Outstanding Facility Features

  • Continuous casting billet furnace with hot top technology for prime quality billets.
  • Aluminium Alloy 6063 homogenized imported primary quality billet.
  • 8 fully computerized automatic extrusion plants with Japanese PLC controls.
  • In-house high precision CNC wire-cut, CNC Milling & EDM machine to make custom designed quality dies.
  • Robotic powder coating plant equipped for wide range of colors.
  • Latest technology packing, stacking and bar coding inventory management system for efficient deliveries.
  • Profile hardness up to 15 Webster standard.
  • Standard profile thickness of 1.2mm and 1.5mm or as per customer’s requirement.
  • Manufactured, supervised and tested under foreign experts.
  • State of the art Italian technology automatic anodizing plant with PULSE technology for silver, bronze, black, golden and S.S. (Champagne) color.
