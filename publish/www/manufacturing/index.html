Our Manufacturing

Precision Manufacturing Across Every Stage

From molten aluminium to the finished profile - every step of the process is engineered for strength, finish and consistency.

01

Casting

Molten aluminium is cast into ingots - the raw material for extrusion

02

Furnace

Ingots are heated in furnaces to optimal extrusion temperature

03

Surface Treatment

Anodizing, powder coating, and wooden finish applied for protection and aesthetics

04

Ingot

Primary aluminium cast into standard ingot sizes for processing

05

Billet

Heated ingots cut into billets ready for extrusion press

06

Profile

Extruded aluminium profiles cut to length and finished

07

Extruding

Billets forced through die to create aluminium profile

08

Powder Coating

Electrostatic powder coating for durable finish in multiple colors

09

Anodizing

Electrolytic passivation to increase corrosion resistance and finish

10

Wooden Finish

Wood grain patterns applied to aluminium surfaces

11

Quality Inspection

BS 1474, ASTM B221, BSTI and AAMA standards verification

12

Packaging

Poly-wrapped and shrink-wrapped with protective tape

13

Warehousing

Bar-coded inventory and nationwide transporter network

14

Tempering

PLC controlled sensors with centrifugal aerodynamic system

15

ETP

Effluent treatment plant - environmentally conscious wastewater treatment

MANUFACTURER OF QUALITY ALUMINIUM PROFILES

DHAKATHAI ALCOMAXX PLC is Bangladesh’s largest single manufacturer of aluminium profiles, operating with eight advanced extrusion presses since 1997.
